AppendixA4

Privacy and safety, by design.

Names, phone numbers and emails removed before any AI sees text
Debrief audio deleted after transcription
No call recordings or transcripts stored from the Navigator
Lock-screen pushes never show case details
Partner sharing only with consent, logged and revocable
Quick exit on every resident screen
Residents see only staff first names
A person confirms every AI suggestion
911 boundary: the Navigator tells callers in danger to hang up and call 911, and never dispatches
Community work stays separate from law enforcement
Privacy is designed in. Names, phone numbers and emails come out before any AI sees text, and debrief audio is deleted after transcription. The Navigator stores no call recordings or transcripts. Pushes never show case details. Partners see a request only with consent, and every share is logged and revocable. A person confirms every AI suggestion. And the Navigator never dispatches: anyone in danger is told to hang up and call 911.
