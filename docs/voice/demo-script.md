# Two-minute live demo

Use synthetic details and an approved teammate as the ComForce responder.
Place the responder in the room with their phone audible but out of view.

## Setup

- Agent display name: **Community Response Navigator**
- Demo caller: teammate A
- Demo responder: teammate B on the only enabled transfer destination
- Scenario: an escalating disagreement that is not currently violent and has
  no weapon, injury, or crime in progress
- Dashboard starts with the synthetic case absent

## Call

Caller: "Two groups in my neighborhood have been arguing online. It isn't
happening right now and nobody has a weapon, but I'm worried it will turn into
a fight tonight. I want someone from ComForce to talk with us."

Expected agent behavior:

1. Identifies itself as AI and gives the 911 boundary.
2. Completes the short immediate-danger gate.
3. Asks only for a general neighborhood and desired help.
4. Finds the reviewed ComForce demo route.
5. Says exactly what minimal summary will be shared and asks consent.
6. Warm-transfers to the approved responder.

Expected responder pre-join summary:

> Caller requests preventive conflict mediation in the north-side demo area.
> They reported no immediate danger, weapon, injury, or event in progress.
> They consented to this handoff.

The responder accepts, greets the caller, and ends after confirming a human
follow-up. The dashboard changes from **requested** to **connected**.

## Presenter narration

"The novelty is not that AI answered a phone. The caller reached us without an
app, the AI preserved the emergency boundary, a human made the consequential
decision, and ComForce can now measure whether help was actually connected—
without storing the recording or a full transcript."

Record a screen capture of this exact synthetic call before finals. If the
